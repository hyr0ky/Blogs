**[HackerOne report #804914](https://hackerone.com/reports/804914)** by `xanbanx` on 2020-02-25, assigned to @cmaxim:

Hi GitLab Security Team,

### Summary

I found a GitLa owned Google docs sheet, which is publicly editable.  In particular, this Google Sheet leaks GitLab's internal swag orders. Moreover, it is editable. Thus, an attacker can change upcoming orders or delete them thus causing financial impact for GitLab. It also can change the shipping address, thus redirecting Swag orders to him.

### Steps to reproduce

Without being logged in to google, visit https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1NrxXRGkSv-3kUzHtJ7Dsd_wWpHgzKfBCRB3wu1YMSvo/edit#gid=0

You see a Google Sheet for GitLab's internal swag orders, which is publicly editable.

### Impact

The linked Google Sheet describes the swag orders for GitLab's events. An attacker can modify them leading to GitLab order no, wrong, or too much swag and thus have financial impact to the marketing team. Also, for example if swag is missing for an event. Furthermore, we can change the shipping contact, thus redirecting swag to the attacker.

### What is the current *bug* behavior?

Google Sheets of GitLab's swag orders is publicly editable.

### What is the expected *correct* behavior?

Proper access control to only allow GitLab team members to access this Google Sheet.

## Report

####  Summary

Hi team, this one is a bit strange and I don't have all the information needed to really assess it. But I thought I would send it in any way so that you can look into it.

About a month back I configured a RSS feed reader in my private Slack that pulls from the gitlab issue tracker. In my first iteration of this I am only monitoring changes in the feed on "closed issues" with the label "security", sorted on "updated"

https://gitlab.com/gitlab-org/gitlab/-/issues/?sort=updated_desc&state=closed&label_name%5B%5D=security&first_page_size=20

Two times this month I have gotten a ping from my server about issues that I can not access. Today I got a ping about this issue

https://gitlab.com/gitlab-org/gitlab/-/issues/418763

![Screenshot_2023-10-10_at_08.29.45.png](https://h1.sec.gitlab.net/a/6b4f13ed-da14-41ff-8624-77279ee0c1dc/Screenshot_2023-10-10_at_08.29.45.png)

Directly after receiving it I tried to visit the issue but got a 404  
![Screenshot_2023-10-10_at_08.38.21.png](https://h1.sec.gitlab.net/a/d75ea7a5-cc52-4bd3-9748-d42473dcdc2f/Screenshot_2023-10-10_at_08.38.21.png)

which means I don't have access to the issue. 

I don't know if this is just a case of some issue being temporarily public and then made private again. But it might be worth looking into the history of that issue and also this one

https://gitlab.com/gitlab-org/gitlab/-/issues/421880

![Screenshot_2023-10-10_at_08.27.40.png](https://h1.sec.gitlab.net/a/ea090747-3fe0-49c1-9b39-2a8bf8f5f2de/Screenshot_2023-10-10_at_08.27.40.png)

Unfortunately (or fortunately) the bot I am using is just posting the link with the title, and there is no way for me "to go back in time" in the feed. Thus I can not show the full leak. But if you visit 

https://gitlab.com/gitlab-org/gitlab/-/issues/?sort=updated_desc&state=closed&label_name%5B%5D=security&first_page_size=20

and click on the three dots (kebab menu) up to the right and click "Subscribe to RSS feed" and open the link in chrome, you can see that the feed actually contains ALL information about the issues, including the full description.  This shows that the issues that I have pointed to which are private would have also been fully exposed at the time of my bot fetching them,

####  Steps to reproduce

This is not really something you can reproduce. Visit https://gitlab.com/gitlab-org/gitlab/-/issues/418763  
 and note that you can not access it. Still, I know the title of the issue from my RSS feed.

Otherwise, start monitoring an RSS feed and wait for it to happen. I will try to recreate this in my own issue tracker, but thought that I would report if first. The easiest for the team is to check the issue linked above and see if it was ever public. 

####  Impact

Leaking confidential issues in the RSS feed will expose hidden (potentially security related) information to the public.

Sorry for this strange report, just wanted to let you know. I will come back here if I find out something relating to this.

Best regards  
Johan

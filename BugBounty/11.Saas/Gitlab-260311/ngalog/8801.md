Details:
**Summary:**
License Management Accept external URL clicking, and that URL accept any schema, such as javascript. It allows attacker to perform XSS on user when browsing pipelines license management tab.

## Quick PoC
Visit https://gitlab.com/golduserngalog/bugbountyref/pipelines/31196139/licenses
Click MIT, click URL, alert box will show up

## Steps To Reproduce:
Fork https://gitlab.com/golduserngalog/bugbountyref/ and trigger CI/CD
And visit the pipeline to look for the license and click license tab and click URL

## Why
